# skeet

**skeet** is an AI-native post-production agent designed for social media creators who want to transform raw, messy footage into polished stories without ever touching a manual timeline. By leveraging **Gemini 3’s multimodal reasoning**, skeet "watches" your clips, understands your creative intent, and automates the entire editing pipeline.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Zero-Timeline Editing**
  Forget dragging and trimming. Upload your raw iPhone or GoPro dumps and describe the "vibe" you want. Skeet handles the heavy lifting of clip selection, trimming, and rhythmic assembly.

- **Interactive Precision (Prompt-to-Segment)**
  Maintain total creative control. Select any section of your footage and provide targeted instructions (e.g., _"Make this specific jump slow-motion with a grainy film filter"_).

- **Multimodal Director Agent**
  Powered by Gemini 3, the agent "watches" your clips to identify high-quality "hero shots" while filtering out shaky, out-of-focus, or accidental footage.

- **Semantic Beat-Matching**
  No more manual snapping to music. Skeet analyzes audio transients and visual energy to ensure every cut aligns perfectly with your chosen soundtrack.

- **Automated Speed Ramping**
  The agent identifies high-action moments and generates complex FFmpeg filter chains to dynamically shift energy and pacing for that professional vlog feel.

- **Vibe-Driven Color Grading**
  Use natural language to define the look. Prompt for "Cinematic," "Vibrant," or "90s Retro" to apply consistent LUTs (Look-Up Tables) across all your clips.

- **Intelligent Audio Ducking**
  Automatically balances background music with dialogue or voiceovers, ensuring your story is always heard clearly over the soundtrack.

---

## 🛠 How It Works

1. **The Scout (Analysis):** You upload a folder of raw clips. Skeet's Director Agent "watches" the footage, tagging moments based on visual quality, movement, and content.
2. **The Intent (Prompting):** You provide a high-level creative direction or select specific segments for custom "Targeted Instructions."
3. **The Assembly (Rendering):** Skeet translates your intent into a sophisticated FFmpeg filter graph, performing the cuts, transitions, and color grading in a high-resolution cloud pipeline.
