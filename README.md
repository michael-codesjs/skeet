🎬 Project Overview: skeet
skeet is an AI-native video editing agent designed for social media creators who want to transform raw, messy footage into polished stories without ever touching a manual timeline. By leveraging Gemini 3’s multimodal reasoning, skeet "watches" your clips, understands your creative intent, and automates the entire post-production pipeline.

🚀 Key Features
Zero-Timeline Editing: Upload raw footage and describe the end goal. Skeet handles the heavy lifting of selection, trimming, and assembly.

Interactive Precision Control: For moments that need a personal touch, users can select specific segments and provide targeted instructions (e.g., "Make this part black and white with slow-motion").

Multimodal Director Agent: Skeet uses native vision reasoning to identify "hero shots" and filter out unusable, blurry, or accidental footage.

Semantic Beat-Matching: Automatically aligns visual cuts to audio transients and musical shifts for a professional rhythmic feel.

Automated Speed Ramping: Skeet identifies high-action moments and generates complex FFmpeg speed-ramp filters to dynamically shift energy.

Vibe-Driven Color Grading: Apply global LUTs via simple prompts like "Cinematic" or "Vintage" to ensure visual harmony across different cameras.

Intelligent Audio Ducking: Automatically balances background music with voiceovers or dialogue so the story is always heard clearly.
